    Echoes of 20/10 | Graceful Thien An 2026 – Moments That Linger

    News & Events
    Thiên Ân Group
    October 21, 2026 (Updated October 23, 2026)

    Share

    No titles in the blog post

    A beautiful 20/10 day has come to an end, but the smiles, the applause and the memorable moments will surely remain in the heart of every Thien An member!

    The programme photo album is now officially live! Let's all look back at the radiant moments and save the lovely photos!

    Duyen Dang Thien An 2026 (Charming Thien An 2026) has closed with so many emotions. From 19 colourful entries to the suspenseful moments of the awards ceremony, everything created a meaningful journey in which the beauty, confidence and charm of Thien An's women shone.

    Congratulations to the outstanding award winners, and our sincere thanks to all the women who took part, the Jury, the Organising Committee and all employees who enthusiastically accompanied and cheered to make the programme a complete success.

    More than the awards, the most wonderful thing is the joy, the bonding and the memories we have created together! Don't forget to save your favourite photos, tag your colleagues and share the lovely moments of the Thien An family!

    Thank you all for a truly brilliant and memorable 20/10 season!
