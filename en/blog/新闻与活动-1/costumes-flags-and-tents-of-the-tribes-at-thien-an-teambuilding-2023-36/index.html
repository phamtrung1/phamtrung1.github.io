    Costumes, Flags and Tents of the Tribes at Thien An Teambuilding 2023

    News & Events
    Thiên Ân Group
    October 16, 2023 (Updated October 23, 2026)

    Share

    Table of contents

    No titles in the blog post

    At Thien An Teambuilding 2023, the tribes enthusiastically presented their unique costumes, flags and tents:

    The Skull Tribespeople finished fourth: The costumes of Tribespeople No. 3 bore a strong regional identity, combining traditional colors with simple elements. Their flag and tent reflected the solidarity and strength of a hunting tribe.

    The flag that finished fourth

    The Binh Minh (Dawn) Tribespeople finished third: The costumes of Tribespeople No. 4 expressed modernity and creativity, combining bold, striking colors with sharp patterns. Their tent served as a resting place and created a lively space for bonding.

    The costumes, flag and tent of the Dawn Tribespeople

    The costumes, flag and tent of Tribespeople No. 4, who finished third

    The Sunshine Tribespeople finished second: The costumes of Tribe No. 2 were radiant with bright colors and unique patterns, expressing the tribe’s cheerful spirit. Their tents were a place to gather and share joy.

    The tribespeople of the Sunshine Tribe

    The costumes, flag and tent of Tribespeople No. 2, who finished second

    The flag of the Hola Tribe with the highest score of the round: The flag of Tribe No. 2 achieved the highest score, with creative design and cheerful colors. It expressed the enthusiasm, dynamism and distinct identity of a conquering tribe.

    The highest-scoring flag of the Hola Tribe

    The costumes, flags and tents of the tribes created a diverse and stylish atmosphere at Thien An Teambuilding 2023.
