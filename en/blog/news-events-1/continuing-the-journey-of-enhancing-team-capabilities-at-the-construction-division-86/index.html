    Continuing the Journey of Enhancing Team Capabilities at the Construction Division

    News & Events
    Thiên Ân Group
    June 16, 2026 (Updated October 23, 2026)

    Share

    Table of contents

    No titles in the blog post

    Continuing the journey of building a learning culture and standardising the management system, on 12/06/2026 Thien An organised an internal training programme for the management team and site staff.

    The programme was attended by Construction Managers, Land Managers, Security – Safety staff, Site Commanders, Deputy Site Commanders and many employees at the project.

    With the theme “Overview of the Quality Management System and Practice in Writing Procedures”, the programme helped the team better understand the role of regulations and procedures in management and operations. The training was delivered by Mr. Chu Quoc Anh – Assistant to the Board of Directors.

    In particular, participants practised building flowcharts and writing procedures using the 5W1H method, step by step standardising ways of working and improving coordination between departments.

    This is one of the important activities contributing to system standardisation, enhanced management capacity and the promotion of digital transformation at Thien An.
