    Civil Construction and Installation Capabilities of Thien An Investment Joint Stock Company

    News & Events
    Thiên Ân Group

    Thien An is a company specializing in professional investment and construction, with over 10 years of experience and dozens of large-scale, high-quality and diverse projects (industrial clusters, urban infrastructure, civil works, transportation works,...)

    With a strong foundation of capabilities, a team of experts in project implementation, management, operation and exploitation, planning in accordance with the law, and a high-tech machinery system, Thien An is directly implementing many leading civil projects in the Northern region. Thien An's operating principle is to deliver the highest-quality products that connect with the community, for the common prosperity of the country. That is also why the company has focused on developing an ecosystem of construction, agriculture and business services to provide complete, all-in-one solutions for construction activities, turning each project into a highlight for the locality and affirming Thien An's mark and high capability.

    Let's take a look at some of Thien An's outstanding civil works

    1. Monument to the “Three Responsibilities” Women's Movement - Dan Phuong, Hanoi

    Project name: Monument to the “Three Responsibilities” Women's Movement

    Location: Center of the Phung Town three-way junction (Road 32 and Road 32 bypass), Dan Phuong District, Hanoi

    Scale: Statue 5,2m high; statue pedestal 3m high; monument 12m high and 2,5m wide; relief 4,5m high and 7m long.

    Materials: The statue and pedestal are made of Thanh Hoa blue stone; the relief base is made of reinforced concrete clad with carved relief stone.

     

    Dan Phuong is the birthplace of the "Three Responsibilities" movement - a vibrant movement that drew millions of women, farmers, workers and doctors across the North to take part for the sake of their brothers and sisters in the South. The "Three Responsibilities" Monument, built and installed by Thien An Investment Joint Stock Company, is a valuable cultural and revolutionary-historical work of Hanoi.

    The work portrays a woman firmly holding a plough and a gun, carrying her child on her back, standing amid ripe rice fields, surrounded by a relief depicting burning American aircraft. The statue embodies the Vietnamese woman on the home front, "indomitable, loyal and resourceful", bearing a simple yet heroic and proud beauty. The work conveys the soul of Vietnamese women and expresses patriotism, with immense spiritual value for educating young generations about the tradition of patriotism and the spirit of fighting for peace. Thien An is greatly honored to have been trusted and selected by the Dan Phuong District Construction Investment Project Management Board to deliver a significant, high-quality civil work, worthy of celebrating the Great Ceremony of the 1.000th Anniversary of Thang Long - Hanoi, leaving its mark on a major cultural and political event of the country and becoming a source of pride for the people of Hanoi in general and of Dan Phuong in particular.

     

    2. Viet Yen District People's Committee Inter-agency Headquarters – Bac Giang

    Project name: Viet Yen District People's Committee Inter-agency Headquarters

    Location: No. 18, Ho Cong Du Street, Zone II, Bich Dong Town, Viet Yen District, Bac Giang Province

     

    The People's Committee is the executive body of the People's Council and the local state administrative agency. The Viet Yen District People's Committee Inter-agency Headquarters was built with the following objectives:

    - Provide a centralized workplace for the district's state administrative agencies

    - Save travel time and costs, facilitating the handling of work

    - Make it easy for citizens to access and work directly with specialized departments

    - Reduce the shortage of land for construction

    The requirements for contractors to undertake public administrative works include planning capability, architectural capability, meeting the technical infrastructure requirements for offices, sustainable urban development, modern equipment and strong management and operational capacity. Thien An has met the stringent standards required of enterprises in the construction and installation field, and the Viet Yen District People's Committee Inter-agency Headquarters is a typical testament to the company's working capacity.

    3. Viet Yen District Central Square, Bac Giang

    Project name: Viet Yen District Central Square, Bac Giang

    Location: In the center of Bich Dong Town, Viet Yen District, Bac Giang Province

     

    Viet Yen District has been recognized as meeting the criteria of a Grade IV urban area, 4 years ahead of the national urban development plan. Through relentless efforts, Viet Yen has taken on a new urban appearance with a series of large-scale, high-quality civil and industrial works that meet the needs of socio-economic development.

    The Viet Yen District Central Square project was implemented by contractor Thien An to fully meet the requirements for architectural space, green grounds and large common activity spaces, as well as handling technical infrastructure relating to the traffic system, power supply, water supply and drainage, and waste treatment. Thanks to Thien An's implementation capacity, the work is regarded as one of the symbols of high community value, enhancing the spiritual life of the people and laying the foundation for the district's business development and cultural exchange.
