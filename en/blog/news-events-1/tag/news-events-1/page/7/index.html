    Northeast Bich Dong New Residential Area – Viet Yen District, Bac Giang: Enhancing the Urban Landscape, Creating Quality Living Conditions for Residents and Improving the Environment

    Alongside in-depth industrial development in the area, Bac Giang Province also places great emphasis on planning, building and implementing new urban and residential area projects to meet the housing needs of the people of Viet Yen, neighboring areas and the industrial parks. Viet Yen District is one of the province's key industrial development localities, home to 3 industrial parks (IPs), 3 industrial clusters (ICs) and craft villages with 487 operating enterprises. Most enterprises in the IPs and ICs have entered stable production and business, providing jobs for about 34 thousand workers. Therefore, the construction of the Northeast Bich Dong Town New Urban Area project will help upgrade the province's urban infrastructure to a spacious, modern standard while improving the quality of life for residents. Thien An Investment Joint Stock Company has won the trust and affection of customers with high-quality construction products and an ecosystem of services that enhance the quality of life for the community. The Northeast Bich Dong New Urban Area project in Viet Yen District is one of the large-scale, high-quality projects directly implemented by Thien An, setting a standard model for building new urban areas in the province.
