    Job description

    * JOB DESCRIPTION

    1. Internal business analysis & solution design

    • Work with the heads of internal business departments to survey and analyse actual operating processes.
    • Model the current (AS-IS) and target optimised (TO-BE) processes; clarify the operational problem, project scope, input/output data, security constraints and acceptance criteria.
    • Evaluate and prioritise software/AI use cases based on business impact, technical feasibility, risks and the Investor's investment costs.
    • Prepare business requirements documents (BRD), overall technical solution documents, data flows, internal implementation plans and KPI sets to evaluate project effectiveness.

    2. Software development, integration & implementation

    • Configure, develop and integrate applications/solutions with the Investor's core systems (ERP, CRM, HRM, DMS, BPM or existing internal applications).
    • Build and work directly with REST APIs, webhooks, JSON, SQL, databases, ETL and data synchronisation mechanisms between departments.
    • Coordinate with the infrastructure/security team to implement centralised authentication (SSO, OAuth), role-based access control (RBAC) and internal data access control.
    • Perform data migration/standardisation, functional/integration testing, coordinate UAT with representatives of business departments, go-live and handover to operations.
    • Monitor system logs, perform root cause analysis, resolve technical incidents and coordinate with the development team/software partners when product changes are needed.

    3. Building & optimising internal AI Agents

    • Analyse business needs to make the right technical decisions: when to develop an AI Agent, when to use a RAG chatbot for internal knowledge lookup, or when to use a traditional automation workflow.
    • Design complete AI Agent architectures: select the language model (LLM), design system prompts, integrate RAG over the enterprise knowledge base, and configure state, memory, processing flows and human approval mechanisms (Human-in-the-loop).
    • Directly program/develop Agents in Python or JavaScript/TypeScript; connect Agents to the Investor's APIs, databases and business software systems.
    • Build tool/function calling, structured output, retry and fallback mechanisms to ensure Agents operate reliably.
    • Design single-Agent or multi-Agent systems depending on the complexity of the internal problem; tightly control the permissions and tools of each Agent.
    • Build an evaluation framework for Agents: measure accuracy, task completion rate, latency and API costs (token cost).
    • Implement guardrails, logging, audit trails, prompt/model version management and monitor the quality of Agent responses in real-world operation.

    4. Internal implementation project management, UAT & go-live

    • Plan and manage schedule, scope, risks, cross-department dependencies and arising change requests.
    • Coordinate between stakeholders: business department representatives (end users), internal IT/Product/Infrastructure teams and external solution/software vendors.
    • Prepare testing checklists, UAT scenarios, internal acceptance records, system configuration documents and operating guides.
    • Closely supervise the go-live phase; prioritise issues, communicate change notifications and directly handle arising incidents.

    5. Training & driving user adoption

    • Survey training needs and segment employees by business role and level of technology proficiency.
    • Develop training plans, lesson plans, demo scripts, hands-on exercises, user guides (SOPs), FAQs and short tutorial videos.
    • Organise training sessions (in person or online); explain software and AI features in simple, easy-to-understand business language.
    • Collect post-training feedback and assess users' learning outcomes to improve materials and delivery methods.
    • Train core staff in each department (Key Users) using a Train-the-Trainer model to support wider rollout in each unit.
    • Track adoption metrics (Adoption Rate, Daily Active Users), identify usability barriers and propose programmes to encourage users to use the applications.

    6. Operations & continuous improvement

    • Prepare reports tracking system quality, error rates, usage levels, operating costs (especially LLM/Cloud costs) and feedback from departments.
    • Propose optimisation solutions for processes, configuration, input data, prompt engineering and Agent architecture based on actual operational data.
    • Standardise documents, processes and implementation templates, and contribute to building the Digital Transformation Knowledge Base for the Group/Company.

    * JOB REQUIREMENTS

    1. Mandatory requirements

    • Experience: At least 02 years of experience in software implementation, system integration, IT solution consulting or an equivalent position.
    • AI Agent experience: Have directly designed and successfully built at least 01 complete AI Agent or POC Agent; able to present the architecture, source code/demo, test scenarios and actual results.
    • Programming skills: Good command of Python or JavaScript/TypeScript; able to read, write, integrate and debug source code independently.
    • Integration foundations: Solid knowledge of REST APIs, webhooks, JSON, SQL, relational/non-relational databases and basic authorisation/authentication security principles.
    • AI/LLM knowledge: In-depth understanding of LLMs, structured prompting, RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation), embeddings, vector search, tool/function calling, structured output, state/memory management and Human-in-the-loop.
    • AI security & control: Able to design AI evaluation (Eval) frameworks and have solutions to control hallucination, prompt injection, enterprise data leakage, and Agents calling the wrong tools or performing actions beyond their authority.
    • Implementation process: Hands-on experience across the full cycle: Business analysis -> Configuration/Development -> Testing -> UAT -> Go-live -> Post-implementation support.
    • Training & communication skills: Have directly trained end users; able to produce clear, high-standard user guides and measure training effectiveness.
    • Qualities & communication: Good listener, logical thinker, clear communicator, proactive problem solver and able to work effectively with non-technical business departments.
    • Education: Bachelor's degree in IT, Computer Science, Management Information Systems (MIS) or a related technical field.
    • Mobility: Willing to travel or work at the Group's construction sites/branches/member companies as required by projects.

    2. Preferred qualifications

    • Experience implementing or integrating with enterprise management systems such as ERP (SAP, Oracle, Odoo...), CRM, HRM, DMS, BPM or B2B SaaS platforms.
    • Experience working with cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, GCP), Linux, Docker, CI/CD, and logging & monitoring systems.
    • Proficient in Agent & automation development frameworks/platforms (such as LangChain, LlamaIndex, LangGraph, AutoGen, n8n, Flowise...) or vector databases (Pinecone, Qdrant, ChromaDB, Milvus...).
    • Knowledge of enterprise information security, internal data classification, personally identifiable information (PII) protection, audit trails and risk management for AI applications in the enterprise.
    • Certification or working experience with the Agile/Scrum model and project management (PMP/Agile).
    • Able to read and understand in-depth technical documentation and communicate in basic English.
