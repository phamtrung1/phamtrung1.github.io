    Planning Manager

    Posted on: 30/06/2026
    Remote

    Apply Now!

    Acts as the link within the business, closely connecting the strategic goals of the Board of General Directors with the actual implementation plans of departments and projects. Ensures optimal use of resources and control of risks and schedules to achieve the highest business performance.

    Customer Relationship
    Personal Evolution
    Autonomy
    Administrative Work
    Technical Expertise

    General information

    SalaryUp to 30 million VND ++ /month
    Work locationHanoi
    Employment typeFull-time, permanent

    Application deadline31/07/2026

    Job description

    * JOB DESCRIPTION

    1. Participate in planning and building a synchronised target-plan management process from Company level to department and member-unit level.

    2. Lead data collection and draft the target plans of the Company, its departments and member units.

    3. Organise the cascading of the Company's target plan to each department and member unit.

    4. Review and appraise the feasibility of department and member-unit plans before submitting them to the Board of Directors for approval.

    5. Inspect, expedite and supervise the implementation of plans by departments and member units

    6. Consolidate data, prepare periodic reports comparing actual results against plans, and report and propose handling, corrective and improvement measures.

    7. Participate in building the key performance indicator (KPI) system for the company, departments and member units

    * JOB REQUIREMENTS

    1. University degree in a relevant field.

    2. Professional experience

    - 05 years of experience in an equivalent position or direct involvement in project management or construction organisation management or related work.

    - Understanding of the implementation sequence of construction investment projects, from preparation and construction to acceptance, handover and commissioning.

    2. Legal knowledge

    - In-depth and broad understanding of the legal framework for construction investment projects (Construction Law, regulations on contracts, planning, as-built documentation, payment and final settlement procedures, etc.).

    3. Financial knowledge:

    - Basic knowledge of corporate finance in a business setting.

    4. Soft skills & technology:

    • Planning & work organisation skills.
    • Communication & negotiation skills
    • A plus: Proficiency in specialised tools such as MS Project, AutoCAD and advanced Excel, or experience using project management/ERP software systems (such as IBOM, FastCons, Base Wework...).

    * BENEFITS

    1. Competitive income up to 30 million VND ++/month

    "Thien An recruits with a commitment to NO SALARY ARREARS, NO LATE SALARY PAYMENTS"

    2. 13th-month salary, holiday and Tet bonuses, performance bonuses.

    3. Access to in-depth professional training programmes.

    4. Lunch allowance at the company canteen.

    5. Rewards: Dedicated reward policy for individuals with outstanding achievements and special contributions.

    6. Insurance: Full health insurance, social insurance and unemployment insurance contributions as required by law upon signing the official labour contract.

    7. Internal welfare:

    • Employee welfare: Birthday benefits and gifts on special occasions (08/03, 20/10, International Men's Day); support for weddings, funerals, illness, etc. for employees and their family members (spouse/children/parents and parents-in-law).
    • Tet gifts for employees' parents aged 80 and over; gifts for employees' children on Children's Day and the Mid-Autumn Festival, and for children with excellent academic results during the year.
    • Vacation and team bonding: Annual company trips, holidays and teambuilding.

    8. Salary review: Annual periodic salary reviews or ad-hoc reviews based on performance.

    9. Business travel allowance: Business travel expenses covered in accordance with company policy.

    10. Leave policy: Public holidays and Tet holidays as prescribed by the State; working hours from Monday to Friday morning plus 2 Saturdays per month.
