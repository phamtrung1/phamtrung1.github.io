    Head of Building Operations Department

    Posted on: 10/09/2026
    Remote

    Apply Now!

    The Head of Operations is fully accountable to the Board of Directors for directing, organizing, managing and controlling the quality of operations, maintenance, security, fire safety (PCCC) and resident services at all high-rise building projects managed by Lavaland in Bac Giang (Vega Homes, Thien An Central,...). This position serves as a strategic bridge between the developer/investor, the Apartment Building Management Board, residents and local state authorities, in order to preserve asset value, maintain a civilized living environment and shape a reputable operations brand for Lavaland.

    Job description

    JOB DESCRIPTION

    1. Developing Processes and Managing the Operations Organization:

    • Prepare the annual overall operations plan and develop a set of standard operating procedures for high-rise building operations.
    • Organize staffing at the Building Management Boards (Vega Homes and Thien An Central management boards), assign tasks, provide professional training and evaluate staff performance (KPIs).
    • Standardize customer care services, and receive and thoroughly resolve residents' requests and complaints in line with quality commitments.

    2. Technical Management, Infrastructure Maintenance & Fire Safety (PCCC):

    • Direct the preparation and implementation of periodic maintenance plans for the building's technical infrastructure: electrical systems, backup generators, water supply and drainage, HVAC, elevators, BMS, fire prevention and fighting (PCCC) systems, and TV/internet.
    • Take primary responsibility for fire safety, occupational safety, and inspection of pressure equipment/elevators in accordance with the law.
    • Regularly inspect the site, and promptly detect and handle urgent technical incidents and risks to structural safety or the building's aesthetics.

    3. Financial Management, Operating Costs & Amenity Operation:

    • Prepare the annual operating budget for each project; control cost norms for common-area electricity and water and consumables.
    • Organize the collection of apartment service fees and parking fees, and recover overdue operating fees to meet the target rate set by the Company.
    • Optimize revenue from value-added services and lawful use of commercial/shared amenity areas to offset operating costs.

    4. Managing Relations with Residents, the Building Management Board & Local Authorities:

    • Coordinate with the developer Thien An to organize the first Apartment Building Conference; represent the operator in signing and executing the Operations Management Service Contract with the Building Management Board.
    • Carry out procedures for the handover/receipt of technical documents, common and private ownership areas, and the maintenance fund (2%) in accordance with the Law on Housing.
    • Represent Lavaland in working directly with Bac Giang provincial state authorities (Department of Construction, Fire Police, ward/city People's Committees) on construction order management, security and public order, and fire prevention and fighting (PCCC).

    5. Managing services:

    • Organize the management of services: Security - Guarding, Industrial Cleaning, Landscaping - Greenery, Elevator Maintenance, and Fire Safety Operations (PCCC).
    • Conduct monthly evaluations of services and make adjustments and improvements to suit building management needs.

    JOB REQUIREMENTS

    1. University degree or higher in Construction Engineering, Mechanical & Electrical (M&E), Urban Management, Architecture, Construction Economics or Business Administration.

    2. Certificate of completion of the professional training course in apartment building operations management as prescribed by the Ministry of Construction; a practicing certificate as an Engineer/Construction Supervisor is preferred.

    3. At least 05 years of experience in real estate operations, including at least 03 years as Head of Operations or Head of the Management Board of a high-rise apartment building with 500 or more apartments.

    4. Valid certificates in Occupational Safety and Health training and in Fire Prevention and Fighting & Rescue.

    5. In-depth knowledge of M&E systems (electrical, plumbing, HVAC, elevators) and fire prevention and fighting (PCCC) in high-rise buildings; ability to direct and approve maintenance plans for technical infrastructure.

    6. Skills in budgeting, controlling common-area electricity and water costs, managing service fee cash flow and optimizing operating costs for the Company.

    7. Skills in communication and negotiating service contracts with the Building Management Board; thoroughly resolving residents' conflicts/complaints; improving satisfaction scores.

    8. Solid knowledge of the Law on Housing, the Law on Construction, Decree 339/2026/NĐ-CP and fire safety (PCCC) regulations; skills in working with Bac Giang provincial state authorities.

    9. Ability to build and standardize the operating process system under the Lavaland brand; control the quality of subcontractors (Security, Cleaning, Landscaping).

    10. Ability to recruit, train, inspire and manage a team of Building Management Board Heads and on-site technical staff.

    11. Ability to respond quickly and directly lead the handling of fires, flooding, total power outages, large-scale disputes or media crises.
