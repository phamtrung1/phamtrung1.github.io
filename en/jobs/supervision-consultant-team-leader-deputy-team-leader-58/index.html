    Supervision Consultant Team Leader / Deputy Team Leader

    Posted on: 09/10/2026
    Remote

    Apply Now!

    The Chief Supervision Consultant holds the highest on-site responsibility for managing and directing the consulting team to ensure that the project is built according to design, meets technical and occupational safety standards, and stays on the schedule committed to the developer/investor .

    Customer Relationship
    Personal Evolution
    Autonomy
    Administrative Work
    Technical Expertise

    General information

    SalaryUp to 30 million VND ++ /month
    Work locationBac Ninh
    Employment typeFull-time, permanent
    GenderNot required
    Number of openings02
    LevelHead of Department
    Experience03
    Application deadline15/07/2026

    Job description

    * JOB DESCRIPTION

    1. Managing the consulting team: Assign tasks to discipline supervisors and control the quality of the whole team's work on site.

    2. Quality and technical supervision: Directly review and approve contractors' construction methods and shop drawings, and ensure that all work items comply with construction regulations.

    3. Schedule control: Urge contractors to follow the plan; report and propose solutions when there are signs of delay.

    4. Acceptance: Chair work acceptance, stage acceptance and completion acceptance of work items; sign and certify contractors' payment and final settlement documents.

    5. Safety and environment: Supervise compliance with regulations on occupational safety, fire and explosion prevention, and environmental hygiene on the construction site.

    6. Handling arising issues: Coordinate with the developer/investor, the design consultant and contractors to resolve technical issues or design changes during construction.

    7. Periodic reporting: Prepare weekly/monthly or ad-hoc supervision reports on project status for the Management Board and the developer/investor.

    * JOB REQUIREMENTS

    1. Education: University degree or higher in Civil and Industrial Construction, Transportation, Hydraulic Engineering or related fields.

    2. Practising certificate: A Grade II or higher construction supervision practising certificate is mandatory.

    3. Experience: At least 03 years of experience in construction/supervision, including at least 01 project in the role of Chief Supervisor or equivalent.

    4. Skills:

    - Excellent team management and project coordination skills.

    - Good communication, negotiation and conflict resolution skills.

    - Proficient in specialized software (AutoCAD, MS Project...) and office applications.

    4. Attitude/Qualities: Honest, decisive in quality management, with a strong sense of responsibility and the ability to handle high work pressure on site.

    * BENEFITS

    1. Competitive income up to 30 million VND ++/month

    "Thien An recruits with a commitment to NO SALARY ARREARS, NO LATE SALARY PAYMENTS"

    2. 13th-month salary;

    3. Bonuses on public holidays, Tet and special Company occasions;

    4. Support with accommodation, living arrangements and meals at the construction site;

    5. Rewards for outstanding achievements;

    6. Health insurance, social insurance and unemployment insurance contributions upon signing an official labour contract;

    7. Internal welfare benefits:

    - Allowances for employees' birthdays and special occasions (March 8, October 20, International Men's Day); weddings, funerals, illness, etc. for employees and their family members (spouse/children/parents and parents-in-law).

    - Tet gifts for employees' parents aged 80 and over; gifts for employees' children on Children's Day and the Mid-Autumn Festival, and for children with excellent academic results during the year.

    - Vacation and team bonding: Annual company trips, holidays and teambuilding.

    8. Annual income review;

    9. Business travel allowance in accordance with Company regulations;

    10. Public holidays and Tet holidays as prescribed by the State;

    11. Opportunities to learn and attend training courses required for the job.
