    Investment / Project Development Specialist

    Posted on: 25/06/2026
    Remote

    Apply Now!

    The Investment Specialist position belongs to the Investment Department of Thien An Investment Joint Stock Company and is responsible for researching, proposing and implementing the Company's real estate investment projects.

    Customer Relationship
    Personal Evolution
    Autonomy
    Administrative Work
    Technical Expertise

    General information

    SalaryUp to VND 25 million++/month
    Work locationHanoi
    Employment typeFull-time
    GenderNot required
    Number of openings02
    LevelSpecialist
    Experience03+ years
    Application deadline31/12/2025

    Job description

    JOB DESCRIPTION

    1. Directly prepare investment project dossiers:

    • Write investment project descriptions (lists of projects calling for investment; lists of projects for auction/bidding; reports/dossiers proposing investment policy approval; other explanatory documents, etc.).

    • Calculate total project investment; land rent/land use fees; prepare the project's financial plan; analyse investment efficiency indicators; other price-related matters.

    2. Work with competent state authorities to process project proposals and other project-related matters.

    3. Planning and reporting: Prepare periodic and ad hoc project reports.

    4. Organise, arrange and archive project records and documents systematically.

    5. Support project development work and related tasks.

    6. Coordinate with and support relevant units in implementing approved investment projects.

    7. Other tasks as assigned.

    CANDIDATE REQUIREMENTS

    1. Education: University degree in Architecture, Construction, Transport, Irrigation, Economics, Law, etc.

    2. Experience: At least 3 years, preferably including 2 years of experience in investment projects.

    3. Knowledge: In-depth understanding of investment, planning, land and construction laws and project calculations.

    4. Skills: Writing project descriptions, project financial calculations, using cost estimation software, proficiency in office applications and AutoCAD.

    5. Attitude: Honest, proactive, willing to learn and cooperate.

    BENEFITS

    1. Competitive income of up to VND 25 million++/month

    "Thien An recruits with a commitment to NO SALARY ARREARS, NO LATE SALARY PAYMENTS"

    2. Bonuses: 13th-month salary, holiday and Tet bonuses, performance bonuses.

    3. Training & development: Opportunities to join in-depth professional training programmes.

    4. Lunch: Lunch provided at the company canteen.

    5. Rewards: Dedicated reward policy for individuals with outstanding achievements and special contributions.

    6. Insurance: Full health insurance, social insurance and unemployment insurance contributions as required by law upon signing the official labour contract.

    7. Internal welfare:

    • Employee welfare: Birthday benefits and gifts on special occasions for employees (8 March, 20 October, International Men's Day); support for weddings, funerals, illness, etc. for employees and their families (spouse/children/parents and parents-in-law).

    • Tet gifts for employees' parents aged 80 and over; gifts for employees' children on Children's Day and the Mid-Autumn Festival, and for children with excellent academic results during the year.

    8. Vacation and team bonding: Annual trips, holidays and team building.

    9. Salary review: Annual periodic income review or ad hoc adjustment based on performance.

    10. Business travel allowance: Business trip expenses covered in accordance with company regulations.

    11. Leave policy: Public holidays and Tet holidays as prescribed by the State; working hours from Monday to Friday morning plus 2 Saturdays per month.

    12. Office: Professional environment, premium office with convenient transport access in the Dan Phuong & Hanoi area.
